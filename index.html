How we work

Four steps, no surprises

You'll know the price and the timeline before anyone picks up a tool.

Step 01

Site visit

We come out, look at the job in person, and talk through what you actually want. No charge, no pressure.

Step 02

Written estimate

An itemised quote in writing — materials, labour and a realistic start date. If something is likely to change, we say so up front.

Step 03

The work

We show up when we say we will, keep the site clean, and check in with you as it goes. Changes get priced before they happen.

Step 04

Walk-through

We walk the finished job with you and fix anything on the list before we invoice. Workmanship is guaranteed.

Questions

Before you call

Do you charge for estimates?

No. We'll come out, look at the job and give you a written estimate at no cost. If the job needs drawings or engineering before it can be priced properly, we'll tell you that up front and what it would cost.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry $2 million liability insurance and we're covered under WCB. Certificates are available on request — just ask.

What area do you cover?

We work throughout Hinton, Edson, Jasper and Yellowhead County, and we're based in Obed, right between Edson and Hinton. If you're a little outside that, ask anyway — depending on the job we can often still help.

Do you take on commercial and facility work?

Yes. Alongside homes and rentals we handle commercial site work and ongoing facility maintenance — concrete aprons and walkways, grounds upkeep, and scheduled repair rounds. Ask about a standing maintenance arrangement if you have a building to keep on top of.

How do deposits and payment work?

Jobs over $1,000 take a 50% deposit up front, with the balance due on completion. Anything under that is invoiced once the work is done. We accept e-transfer, cash or cheque.
